# Rollback Procedure

Document how to safely revert an unsuccessful deployment.

## Trigger conditions

Describe the signals or symptoms that indicate a rollback is necessary.

## Rollback steps

1. Confirm the failure and scope of impact.
2. Identify the last known good deployment.
3. Revert or redeploy the previous stable version.
4. Verify application health and user impact.
5. Record lessons learned and follow-up actions.
